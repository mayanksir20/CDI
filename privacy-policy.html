Behind the Scenes:
Transparency in Action

Operational Efficiency

Hum aapka data sirf website chalane ke liye nahi, balki apni services ko behtar banane ke liye use karte hain. Jaise ki aapke feedback se hum apne **Eco-friendly Resorts** ki quality aur **Canteen management** ko upgrade karte hain.

Strategic Communication

CDI ki **Strategy & Methodology** updates aap tak pahunchane ke liye hum aapka email use karte hain. Hum spamming mein believe nahi karte, sirf kaam ki information hi share ki jati hai.

"Data security is not just a policy for us; it's a promise to our partners and clients across all CDI verticals."

Data Collection

At CDI, we collect information to provide better services to our partners and clients.

  • Personal Details: Name, email, and phone via contact forms.
  • Usage Data: IP address and browser type for site optimization.
  • Purpose: To enhance our hospitality and eco-resort management.

Data Protection

Security is at the heart of our methodology. We use industry-standard encryption to keep you safe.

  • No Data Selling: We never trade or sell your information to third parties.
  • Legal Safety: Data is only shared when required by law or safety protocols.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data. We believe in transparency and user empowerment.

  • Access: Request a copy of your data anytime.
  • Cookies: Manage cookie preferences via browser settings.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from strategy updates with one click.

Commitment to Transparency

CDI (Creative Development & Infrastructure) is committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected. Should we ask you to provide certain information by which you can be identified when using this website, then you can be assured that it will only be used in accordance with this privacy statement.

Our partnership and networking strategy involves collaborating with various stakeholders. We ensure that all our data processing activities are aligned with global standards to deliver positive impact and outcomes.
